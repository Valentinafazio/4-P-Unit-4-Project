Come Visit Bergen Beach Brooklyn!

This guide contains all my favorite spots I like to go to around my neighborhood!

Frozen Planet

Frozen Planet is the neighbors local frozen yogurt shop that everyone goes to. They have a bunch of flavors of yogurt and the biggest selection of toppings I've ever seen!



Here are some of my favorite frozen Yogurt combos:

  • Cake batter yogurt with oreos, mini cookie dough bites and mini cake bites
  • Mango frozen yogurt with mangos, cherries, and strawberry popping boba

Panino Rustico

This is my favorite Panini Place! They make the best Panini's, pasta and the workers are always the nicest!



Aviator feilds

Aviator feilds used to be an old airport and now is used to host Xavierian High School, a Brooklyn Catholic schools football games. Me and my friends like to go check out the games when we have sleepovers or when we are bored. Recently, the city announced that they're going to be doing construction at Aviator and transform the area into an arts center.
