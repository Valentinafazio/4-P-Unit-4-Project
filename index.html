Frozen Planet!

Frozen Planet is the neighbors local frozen yogurt shop that everyone goes to. They have a bunch of flavors of yogurt and the biggest selection of toppings I've ever seen!



Here are some of my favorite frozen Yogurt combos:

  • Cake batter yogurt with oreos, mini cookie dough bites and mini cake bites
  • Mango frozen yogurt with mangos, cherries, and strawberry popping boba
